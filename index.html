    Getting started

    ObbyTrap Factions is a 1.8 factions server: build a base, grind money, enchant your gear and blow up everyone else's walls.

    Joining

    • Address: obbytrap.net
    • Join with Minecraft 1.8, or with 1.13.1 and anything newer. Versions 1.9 through 1.13 can't connect.
    • The server runs 1.8 rules whatever you join with. Swords have no attack cooldown, and blocking with a sword halves the damage you take.
    • You never get hungry.

    Your first ten minutes

    1. Type /kit trapped for diamond armour, a sword, tools, 232 obsidian, 32 TNT and food. You start with $5,000.
    2. Type /rewards and take the daily reward: $10,000, 128 obsidian and a crate key.
    3. Type /redeemstart and /redeemsetup. Between them they give $250,000, two crate keys and enough free sand to fill two sandbots. Each works once a month.
    4. Type /wild and pick a distance to get away from spawn.
    5. Make a faction with /f create <name>, claim the ground you are standing on with /f claim, and set a home with /f sethome.
    6. Start earning. Sugar cane, cactus and spawners are the usual ways. See Money and the shop.

    Finding your way

    • /help opens the guide menu, /menu your profile and shortcuts, and /rules the rules.
    • At spawn, each character and sign opens one part of the server when you click it: the shop, enchantments, mob coins, daily rewards, wild, the battle pass, the bank, the bar, coin flip, generators, gen buckets and the richest-players list.
    • /warp opens the list of places to go: crates, KOTH, outpost, stardrop, totem, end and nether.
    • The scoreboard on the right shows your rank, money, power, mob coins and your faction's leader and claims.

    Typing commands Press Tab while typing a command to see the commands you can use and to finish the one you have started.

    Factions: the basics

    A faction is your team. You claim land together, build a base on it, and raid other factions' bases with TNT cannons.

    Starting or joining one

    • /f create <name> makes a faction. The name is 3 to 10 characters.
    • /f invite <player> invites someone, and they type /f join <name>. Factions are invite-only unless the leader opens them.
    • /f leave leaves. /f who <name> shows any faction's members, power and land, and /f list lists them all.
    • A faction holds up to 15 members. Alt accounts are kept on a separate list of up to 30, managed with /f alts.
    • A member who hasn't logged in for 90 days is removed from their faction.

    Home and warps

    • /f sethome sets the faction home. It has to be on your own land. /f home takes you there at once, and you respawn there when you die.
    • /f setwarp <name> adds a faction warp, with a password if you want one. /f warp opens the menu of them. A faction can have up to 5.
    • Neither works while you are in combat.

    Allies and enemies

    • Every faction starts out neutral to yours. /f ally <faction>, /f enemy <faction> and /f neutral <faction> change that. An alliance needs both sides to agree.
    • You get one ally. There are no truces.
    • Names are coloured by how they stand to you: your own faction, an ally, an enemy, and white for neutral.
    • You can hurt anyone who is not in your faction or allied to it, neutral players and players with no faction included.

    Faction chat

    • /f chat faction, /f chat ally and /f chat public switch where your messages go.
    • /f global hides public chat for you.
    • /f coords sends your location to your faction. /f weewoo sounds the alarm that you are being raided.

    Shared storage

    • /f chest opens a chest the whole faction shares. It grows with the Chest upgrade. Spawners can't go in it.
    • /f tnt add <amount> and /f tnt take <amount> move TNT in and out of the faction's TNT bank, which holds up to 3,000,000.
    • /f invsee <member> shows a member's inventory.

    Flying

    • /f fly lets you fly on your faction's land, and on your ally's land if they allow it in their permissions.
    • It takes 3 seconds to start and switches off when an enemy comes within 32 blocks.
    • You can't throw ender pearls while flying, and you can't start flying while in combat.

    Not on this server Faction banners and the faction vault are turned off. The faction bank doesn't charge for anything: making a faction, claiming land and setting a home are free.

    Power and land

    Power decides how much land a faction can hold. Run out of it and your land can be taken.

    Power

    • You start with 100 power, which is also the most you can have. The least is -10.
    • Dying costs 4 power, wherever it happens.
    • You get 0.2 back every minute you are online, which is 12 an hour. Nothing comes back while you are offline.
    • A faction's power is its members' power added up. /f lowpower lists members who are below their maximum.

    Claiming

    • Land is claimed a chunk at a time (16 by 16 blocks, from the bottom of the world to the top). Each chunk needs 1 power.
    • /f claim claims the chunk you are standing in. /f map shows the area, and you can click chunks on it to claim them. /f unclaim gives a chunk up.
    • Your chunks don't have to touch each other.
    • /f corner, typed in a corner chunk of the world, claims a 25 by 25 chunk square from that corner in one go.
    • /f showclaims lists what you hold, and /f seechunk draws the edges of the chunk you are in.
    • You can't claim around spawn or the KOTH.

    Losing land

    • If your faction holds more chunks than it has power, an enemy can claim your land for themselves, starting from its edge.
    • That takes a lot of deaths: a faction of five has 500 power.
    • Leaving a faction is allowed even when your power is below zero.

    What your land protects

    • Players outside your faction can't place or break blocks on it, or use buckets, flint and steel or armour stands there. That holds whether you are online or not.
    • Doors, levers, buttons, chests, hoppers and the like follow /f perms. Open it and check what each group may do, containers above all.
    • Explosions are not blocked. TNT and creepers break blocks on claimed land at any hour. That is what raiding is.
    • Beacons on your land can't be broken by outsiders. Endermen can't move blocks on it.
    • A player in enemy, neutral or warzone land can't set an Essentials home there.

    Cannons and TNT

    Bases are raided with TNT cannons. Explosions break blocks on claimed land whether the owners are online or not.

    TNT

    • /f tntfill <radius> <amount> fills the dispensers around you with TNT. The radius goes up to 50 blocks and each dispenser takes up to 300.
    • The faction TNT bank (/f tnt add, /f tnt take) keeps TNT out of your chests.
    • TNT does no damage in the safezone or the warzone.
    • Creepers break blocks on claimed land too, but not in the warzone or around spawn.
    • Fire doesn't spread anywhere.

    Grace period

    At the start of a map there is a grace period. While it lasts, TNT and falling sand are switched off, so nobody can be cannoned. PvP stays on. /grace says whether it is still running.

    Limits

    These keep one cannon from stalling the server. A normal cannon never meets them, and you are told in chat when one stops something.

    WhatLimitPast the limit
    TNT fired from one chunk's dispensers3,000 in 4 secondsThe rest stays in the dispensers. A volley fires whole or not at all.
    Falling sand in one chunk1,500 in 4 secondsThe sand stays where it is.
    Redstone updates in one chunk20,000 in 10 secondsThe chunk's redstone pauses for 30 seconds. Chunks near a cannon that has fired in the last 15 minutes are never paused.
    Piston moves in one chunk2,000 in 10 seconds
    Hoppers in one chunk64No more can be placed.
    Hopper minecarts in one chunk8No more can be placed.

    Water and wiring

    Water and lava stop at redstone, repeaters, comparators, torches, levers, buttons, rails, carpets and flowers instead of washing them away, so a cannon can run water past its wiring. Wheat, carrots and potatoes are still broken by water, so water-harvested farms work.

    Ender pearls

    • 15 seconds between pearls, and one in the air at a time.
    • You can't pearl while your head is inside a block, for 2 seconds after opening a door, across the world border, or into spawn while in combat.

    Read first Some cannon designs are banned. See Rules before you build one.

    Sandbots

    A sandbot keeps a cannon's sand columns full, so nobody has to stand there restacking sand.

    1. Put a sandstone block at the top of each sand column of your cannon.
    2. Look at a block next to the cannon, within 6 blocks, and type /sandbot place. The bot appears in the space in front of that block.
    3. Right-click the bot and choose Buy sand. Sand costs $1 each and a bot holds up to 100,000. If you can't afford a full load, you buy what you can.
    • Whenever the space under a sandstone within 16 blocks of the bot is empty, the bot drops a sand there. One bot can feed every column of a cannon, and a column that is already full uses nothing.
    • It works while someone from your faction is within 64 blocks, or while it sits inside a running chunk loader's area.
    • It goes on your faction's land or in the wilderness. A faction can have 10, and members and up can place and manage them.
    • The bot's menu also pauses it and removes it. Removing takes two clicks, and the sand left in it is lost.
    • It can't be pushed or blown up, and other factions can't open it. In the wilderness, anyone can break it with two right-clicks, and you are told who did.
    • When a bot runs out of sand, members nearby are told.
    CommandWhat it does
    /sandbot placePlaces a bot in front of the block you are looking at
    /sandbot listLists your faction's bots and the sand each has left
    /sandbot refillBuys sand for the nearest bot
    /sandbot removeRemoves your nearest bot within 8 blocks. Type it twice to confirm.
    /sandbot remove allRemoves every bot your faction has. Type it twice to confirm.

    /f sandbot does the same as /sandbot.

    Chunk loaders

    A chunk loader keeps a cannon running after everyone has walked away from it.

    • It is a beacon you place on your faction's own land. The 5 by 5 chunks around it keep running with nobody near: redstone, dispensers, pistons, lit TNT, falling sand, and any sandbots inside.
    • Shots fired from inside its area keep flying after they leave it, so they land where the cannon is aimed.
    • /chunkloader opens the shop. A loader costs $25,000.
    • It runs on time you buy for it: $1,000 an hour, up to 48 hours stored. A new loader has none, so right-click it and buy time first. The same menu pauses it. Time only counts down while it is running, and members online are told when it runs out.
    • Pick it up from its menu (two clicks) or by breaking it. You get the item back with its time still in it.
    • A faction can have 3 placed at once. Members and up can place and manage them. /chunkloader list shows yours.
    • Explosions can't break it. If the land under it is unclaimed or taken, it stops, and whoever can build there can destroy it. If the faction disbands, its loaders are gone.

    Raid alerts

    When TNT from somewhere else goes off on your land, everyone online in your faction is told at once.

    • The alert comes with a title and a heartbeat sound, and says where you were hit, how much TNT landed in how long, which direction it came from, and how many blocks you lost, counting spawners and obsidian separately.
    • If most of the TNT came from one faction's land, the alert names that faction.
    • Click the last line for a map centred on the raid, with the chunks being hit marked by a red X. /raids map opens the same map.
    • While the hits go on you get a fresh alert every 30 seconds. After 90 seconds without a hit you are told it has stopped, with the totals.
    • Hits more than 2 chunks apart count as separate raids, so a base hit from two sides gets two alerts.
    • Your ally is told when a raid on you starts.
    • Log in during a raid and you get the alert with the totals so far. If your land was hit while you were offline, you get a one-line reminder.
    • /raids lists the last 10 raids on your faction. The list starts fresh after a server restart.

    Not counted as a raid: TNT that started on your own land, creepers and TNT minecarts, and anything in the safezone or warzone.

    The raid event

    Every 4 hours a fortified base goes up in a world of its own. The first faction to get TNT into its vault has the loot to itself.

    When

    • At 12am, 4am, 8am, 12pm, 4pm and 8pm, server time.
    • Warnings go out 30, 10, 5 and 1 minutes before. At the start everyone gets a title and a chat line to click.
    • Between events, /raid says when the next one is.

    Getting in

    • You need to be in a faction. /raid takes you to the arrival point, south of the base.
    • You can't be hurt for 10 seconds after arriving, unless you attack someone first.
    • /raid is the only way in. Homes, /tpa and /back don't reach the raid world. If you die or leave, type /raid again.
    • The world is flat, with a border 450 blocks from the base.

    Rules of the world

    • No building, breaking or buckets within 32 blocks of the base, in the corners between its walls, or within 6 blocks of the arrival point.
    • Only TNT breaks blocks there. Creepers and TNT minecarts don't.
    • Your faction can claim up to 9 chunks for its cannon. Members and up can claim, and this land never counts against your power. It can't be taken from you, and you can't claim right next to another faction's cannon.
    • Sandbots and chunk loaders work there, outside the no-build area, with no claim needed. Faction homes and warps don't.
    • Fights there don't set off raid alerts.

    Winning

    • The vault is won by the first TNT that goes off inside it or breaks a piece of it. It goes to the faction that placed the dispenser the TNT came from.
    • From then on only the winners can step into the vault or open its 20 chests. Everyone else is pushed back out. PvP carries on everywhere else.
    • The winners get 10 minutes. Then everyone is sent to spawn.
    • If nobody breaches the vault within 60 minutes, nobody wins.

    Afterwards

    The world is wiped and the base rebuilt for next time. Cannons, items and claims there are gone. Sandbots left there are removed and their unused sand is refunded to your faction's leader. Chunk loaders left there are handed back to whoever placed them, with their time.

    Money and the shop

    Money buys spawners, tools, enchants and faction upgrades. You start with $5,000.

    The basics

    • /bal shows your money, /pay <player> <amount> sends some, and /baltop lists the richest players.
    • /withdraw <amount> turns money into a bank note you can hand to someone or keep in a chest. Right-click the note to cash it. From $25 to $10,000,000, with no fee.
    • /xpbottle <amount> does the same with experience, also free. /bank has buttons for the common amounts of both.

    Auction house

    • /ah opens it. Hold an item and type /ah sell <price> to list it at a fixed price, or /ah bid <starting price> to auction it.

    • Shift and right-click a player within 15 blocks to ask for a trade. They accept the same way, or with /trade accept. A request lasts 60 seconds.
    • Items, money and experience can all go into a trade.

    Custom enchants

    81 enchants beyond the vanilla ones, in four tiers. They come as books you drop onto your gear.

    Getting and using books

    1. Type /ce, or click the enchantments character at spawn.
    2. Buy a lost book with experience. It costs experience points, not levels: Common 1,500, Rare 5,000, Ultimate 15,000, Legendary 30,000.
    3. Right-click the lost book. It turns into a random enchant book of that tier.
    4. Pick the book up in your inventory and drop it onto the item.
    • An item holds 5 custom enchants. Vanilla enchants don't count towards that.
    • A book of a higher level replaces the lower level already on the item.
    • The blacksmith, in the same menu, merges two items or two books for experience levels: 7 to move an enchant across, 10 to raise one both items share, 15 to combine two identical books into the next level.
    • The tinkerer (/tinkerer) takes enchanted gear and books you don't want and pays you in bottled experience.

    PvP is on everywhere except inside spawn.

    Combat tag

    • Hitting a player or being hit tags you both for 15 seconds.
    • Log out while tagged and you die.
    • While tagged you can't walk or pearl into spawn.
    • These don't work while tagged: /f home, /f warp, /f fly, /f leave, /spawn, /home, /warp, /back, /tpa, /tpaccept, /wild, /pv, /ec, /sell, /fix.
    • You can still place and break blocks, throw pearls and craft.
    • /logout logs you out safely after 10 seconds of standing still. /f logout does the same in 30.

    What changes from vanilla

    • After an enchanted golden apple, you can't eat another for 4 minutes. The time left shows above your hotbar.
    • Ender pearls have a 15-second cooldown.
    • Strength adds less damage than in vanilla.
    • Blocking with your sword halves the damage you take.
    • Enchanted golden apples can't be crafted. They come from kits, crates and the shop.
    • For 15 seconds after a kill, only the killer can pick up what dropped.
    • You can't deal damage for the first 3 seconds after logging in.

    Dying

    • You drop everything and lose 4 power.
    • You come back at your faction home if you have one.
    • A death message goes to the whole server. For 30 seconds, anyone can click your name in it to see what you were carrying.
    • If you die in the wilderness, your head drops.
    • /ks shows your kills, deaths, streak and ratio. Killing the same player more than 3 times in 5 minutes stops counting.

    Rules

    Be respectful, be fair, and use common sense. /rules shows these in game.

    Chat

    • No spam. Three or more messages in a row counts.
    • No racism.
    • No advertising. It is an immediate ban.
    • No links.
    • Dodging a mute on another account is a 24-hour IP mute.

    Fair play

    • No hacked clients, x-ray or auto-fishing.
    • No duplicating items. Report a bug you find and you are rewarded for it.
    • No lag machines.
    • No insiding: joining a faction to rob or betray it.
    • Scamming with in-game items and money is allowed, so trade carefully. Scamming over real money is not. Real-money trades themselves are allowed.
    • No home-made raid alarms, such as ones built on block sounds. The server's own raid alerts do that job.
    • Redstone check boxes are allowed.

    Cannons

    • No cannons that one-shot two walls.
    • No left or right shooters.
    • No wall removers.
    • No phase cannons.
    • Fully automatic cannons are allowed below Y 10.
    • Semi-automatic cannons are allowed, without automatic sand compressors.
    • Roof cannons are allowed, with restrictions. Ask staff before building one.

    Bases

    • 5 alt accounts per faction at most.
    • No claiming single chunks around someone's base to block or annoy them. You get one warning, then all your land is unclaimed.
    • On a new map, the faction that claims the very corner chunk owns that whole corner.
    • No watering spawners, and no water systems.
    • No obsidian over spawners.
    • No chests, cobwebs or iron bars in or under walls.

    Built-in limits

    • Chests, repeaters, comparators and doors can't be placed next to cactus, sugar cane, mushrooms or lily pads.
    • Pistons can't push ores, glowstone, netherrack, end stone or packed ice.
    • The roof of the nether can't be reached.
    • Books stop at 50 pages.
    • /plugins, /version and commands with a colon in them are blocked.

    Staff and reports

    • Staff ranks, lowest to highest: Watcher, Sentinel, Architect, Overseer. /list shows which are online.
    • /report <player> <reason> reports someone who is online. Add a screenshot link if you have one (Imgur, Gyazo, prnt.sc). You can have 3 reports open, a minute apart.
    • When staff close a report that held up, you get a thank-you note and a diamond block. If you are offline at the time, collect it with /reports claim.
    • If staff freeze you, you can't move, fight, build or use commands until they are done. Do what they ask. It usually means joining Discord for a screenshare.
    • Punishments are warnings, kicks, mutes and bans, by account or by IP. To appeal one, ask on Discord: discord.gg/obbytrap
